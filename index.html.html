1XBET Wallet Agent Program Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you apply.

Answers About Registration, Verification, and Daily Agent Work

This FAQ page answers common questions about wallet agent programs, including application requirements, access, verification procedures, commissions, and daily responsibilities. If you are comparing this opportunity with other online income options, these answers can help you understand the general workflow, support process, and payment model.

This is an independent informational website. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by 1XBET. Verify the legitimacy of any opportunity and all requirements through independently confirmed official channels before sharing personal information or making payments.

Do I need to talk to players?

No. Players deposit directly and submit requests through the management site. You only confirm requests.

What do I need to start?

Verification, a smartphone, and a working balance. After approval, you’ll receive secure access details and installation files for the agent applications.

How is commission paid?

Commission is credited daily. Standard rates are 7% for deposits and 2% for withdrawals.

What is a 1XBET retail agent?

A 1XBET retail agent, also known as a 1XBET wallet agent or e-wallet agent, acts as a local payment partner. They handle deposits and withdrawals for users in their country using the secure retail app, without needing to interact with players directly.

Where can I download the 1XBET agent app?

Once your application is approved and verified by our support manager, you will receive secure access links, credentials, and installation files for the 1xBetManagement and Reddy applications.

How do I apply?

Use the contact form and share your name, email, country, phone number, and Telegram ID. Our onboarding manager will contact you.

What is the minimum initial working balance required?

There is no fixed minimum balance set by the system, but we recommend starting with a small working balance (around $3000 to $10000 equivalent in your local currency) to learn the transaction confirmation flow and build a routine.

Can I log in to the agent app on multiple devices simultaneously?

No. For security and fraud prevention, your agent account is tied to a single device hardware ID. If you need to switch devices, you must contact your onboarding manager to reset your device pairing.

How long does the verification process usually take?

Review usually starts within 1 to 2 hours after submission. Manager contact is typically made the same day, and portal access with installation files is usually shared within 2 to 3 working days after verification.

Are there any hidden fees or charges for agents?

No. The 1XBET agent program is entirely free to join. There are no registration fees, monthly subscription costs, or software charges. Your earnings come purely from the deposit (7%) and withdrawal (2%) commissions.

Can I work as an agent if I already have a personal 1XBET betting account?

Yes. However, your agent profile must be kept completely separate from your personal betting account. The agent app uses a dedicated login portal and balance ledger that does not intersect with regular player accounts.

What should I do if a player provides a wrong transaction ID?

If the player's transaction ID or receipt does not match your local payment system ledger, do not approve the request. Flag it in the system and instruct the player to contact the support team with their official payment proof.
